Which Edition Is Right for Your Business?

Answer a few operational questions to find your optimal PrimeDesk deployment setup.

Choose Desktop Edition if:

  • • You operate a physical retail counter where checkout speed is critical.
  • • Your internet connection is intermittent or you want 100% offline immunity.
  • • You use thermal receipt printers (58mm/80mm) and high-speed USB barcode scanners.
  • • You want to connect multiple cashier counter terminals over local shop Wi-Fi without cloud lag.
  • • You want all data stored exclusively on your own premises in encrypted SQLite.

Choose Online Cloud Edition if:

  • • You need to check sales, inventory, and customer ledgers from anywhere via laptop or tablet.
  • • You love working with Google Sheets and want your raw business data stored in your Google Drive.
  • • You don't want to install software on individual employee PCs.
  • • You run wholesale distribution or field sales operations on mobile devices.
  • • You prioritize automatic cloud synchronization and zero-maintenance backups.
